## 🔍 Reverse-Engineering SEC Form D Filings for Warm Intros

Founders often waste hundreds of hours cold pitching dormant funds. Public regulatory filings allow you to legally uncover who is actively writing checks:

1. **Item 7 - Type of Filing**: Check if a fund filed a `New Notice` vs `Amendment` in the last 90 days. A new notice signals freshly closed LP capital that **must be deployed**.
2. **Item 13 - Offering Amount**: Uncovers the exact dollar amount closed in the LP vehicle. If total offering amount equals total amount sold, the fund has finished capital formation and is in active deployment mode.
3. **Item 3 - Related Persons**: Reveals named Managing Directors, General Partners, and Executive Officers who sign regulatory documents with personal liability. These are the true decision-makers.

For a full step-by-step masterclass with Python extraction scripts and regex patterns, read the [SEC Form D & ADV Intelligence Guide](https://zinvestorhunting.com/guides/sec-form-d-adv-intelligence-guide).
